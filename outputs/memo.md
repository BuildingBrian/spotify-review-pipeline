# Recommendation: Focus next quarter on billing/support, starting with free-tier restrictions

## Why
- billing.premium_only_restrictions ranks #2 overall with 9469 complaints [C1] and a severity sum of 26088 [C2]. Its mean severity is 2.755096 [C3].
- The only issue ranked above it, other.general_criticism, is a non-specific catch-all (severity sum 40494 [C4]) and offers no actionable target.
- At topic level, billing accounts for 18.1% of complaints, and it has the most cancellation-intent reviews of any topic (2419), well above usability (1249) and playback (517). This is expressed intent, not observed churn.
- The restrictions issue outranks every access and playback issue on priority score. For comparison, access.login_failure scores 7340 [C5].

## Representative customer evidence
- 3bee1604-8014-4a7d-86fd-54bf00a0e85f: "using the app without the membership is not fun. It will play songs that you don't have on your playlists, as well as constant ads"
- 221709a4-4026-4324-a13c-ad80e23d85d9 (cancellation intent): "will look for alternative apps or platforms instead of Spotify"
- 257eb4dc-0261-444b-8af0-6316558c08ca: "on phone it's so limited without premium. Only 6 skips per hour, you can't adjust the place on the song"

## Alternatives considered
- **Usability**: the largest topic by volume, but mostly lower severity. usability.ads_frequency has a priority score of 17269 [C6]. It overlaps with the free-tier complaints and could be addressed alongside them.
- **Access**: login failure is the most severe issue (mean severity 3.910495 [C7]), but its volume is small at 1877 complaints [C8]. It is a strong candidate for a targeted fix, though not the main investment.
- **Playback**: playback.stops_or_pauses scores 7169 [C9]. Severity is high, but the scale is smaller than the billing issue.

## Limitations
These are self-selected public Google Play reviews from May 2022 to Nov 2023, drawn from a classified sample. The pack has no revenue, plan-tier or churn data. Cancellation language is expressed intent only. Billing here mostly reflects free-tier friction, not payment errors, and support volume is tiny. The classification and severity scores are model-assigned.
